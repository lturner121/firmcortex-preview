1 Who we are and what this policy covers

Firmcortex LLC, based in New York, New York, operates the FirmCortex marketing website at firmcortex.ai and other marketing pages that link to this Privacy Policy. In this policy, “FirmCortex,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to Firmcortex LLC.

This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit these pages, request a demonstration or contact us about our business.
